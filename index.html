Privacy Policy

How AKMoney handles your information

AKMoney — Budget & Expense Tracker is a local-first app. Your financial records are kept on your device and are not automatically sent to AKSoft Apps. If you use receipt scanning, Google's ML Kit library may send technical diagnostics to Google. This policy explains what the app does.

Last updated October 16, 2026

Key principles

A plain-language summary. The numbered sections below are the full policy and take precedence.

  1. Your records stay on your device. Transactions, accounts, budgets, goals and receipts are stored in the App's private storage. AKMoney has no accounts and no cloud sync, and no AKSoft Apps server receives your financial data.
  2. No advertising, and no analytics or crash reporting of our own. The one technical exception is Google's ML Kit library, used for receipt scanning (Premium), which may send diagnostic and usage data to Google. See Section 3.
  3. Receipt text recognition runs on your device. Google's published ML Kit disclosure lists technical data, not receipt images or recognised text. It does not describe the exact behaviour of every library version (Section 3).
  4. You control what leaves your device. Backups, reports, exports, support messages and milestone cards go only where you send them.
  5. Other network use is limited. The App checks Google Play for your Premium status and, when you ask, for updates. Voice entry uses only your device's on-device recognizer, with no online fallback.

Overview

1Overview & Permissions

This policy explains how AKMoney ("the App"), published by AKSoft Apps ("we," "us"), handles information. AKMoney keeps your financial data on your device. It has no accounts and no cloud sync, and AKSoft Apps does not receive your financial data. We use the data stored in the App only to run its features on your device, never for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

The App does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services of its own. The only technical exception is the diagnostic data that Google's ML Kit library may send to Google when you use receipt scanning (Section 3).

The App itself declares permissions for network access, biometric unlock, notifications, restarting the daily reminder after a reboot, and the microphone (voice entry only). Bundled libraries add standard permissions for checking network state, vibration, keeping the device awake and running scheduled background work, and Google Play Billing. The App does not declare camera, contacts, location or storage permissions. Receipt photos can be captured using your device's camera app or selected from your gallery.

2Information Stored Locally

The App stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Financial records: expenses, income, accounts, transfers, budgets, recurring transactions, categories, tags and goals, kept separately in each profile you create.
  • Profiles: names, currency settings and avatars (built-in, or a photo you choose from your device).
  • Receipts and attachments: images you add to an expense, and the receipt details you review and save (Section 8).
  • Preferences and templates: theme, reminder settings, the optional display name you enter, and transaction templates.
  • Financial Journey progress: experience points, levels, streaks and achievements, calculated from your own activity.
  • App Lock: a one-way verifier of your PIN, if you set one (Section 6).

None of this is transmitted automatically. Each profile's financial database is encrypted on your device. Receipt images and profile photos are separate files that are not separately encrypted.

Third parties and data transmissions

3Third-Party Services & Telemetry

The App does not automatically send your financial records to AKSoft Apps or to anyone else. These are the third-party services and network requests involved:

  • Google ML Kit (receipt scanning, Premium). Receipt text recognition is performed on your device using Google's ML Kit library, which is bundled in the App. The App does not send the receipt image or the recognised text to AKSoft Apps or to a cloud text-recognition service. Google's published ML Kit disclosure describes technical diagnostic and usage data that may be sent to Google, such as device and app information (model, operating-system and app version), per-installation identifiers, and performance and error details. It states that this data is sent over HTTPS and is not transferred to third parties, and it does not list receipt images or recognised text among the data described. That disclosure covers ML Kit generally, has no section specific to text recognition, and does not establish the exact behaviour of every version of the bundled library. We declare this data in Google Play's Data Safety section as Diagnostics and Device or other IDs, collected for Analytics. It is collected by Google's library rather than by AKSoft Apps, and the App has no setting to turn it off. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing. The App asks Google Play whether the Premium product is available and whether you have Premium (Section 11). No financial data is included.
  • Update check. Only when you choose "Check for Updates" in Settings, the App asks Google Play whether a newer version exists. It downloads and installs nothing itself.
  • Share sheet and file picker. When you share or save a report, backup, support message or milestone card, the destination you choose (an app, a local folder or a cloud-storage provider) receives it. AKMoney does not control that destination or receive your credentials for it.
  • Speech recognition. Voice entry uses your device's own on-device recognizer (Section 5).

Google receives the standard technical information that accompanies any such request, such as your IP address.

4Support Requests & Feedback

The App has no crash-reporting service, writes no error-log file of its own, and sends no reports automatically. Like most Android apps, it may emit diagnostic messages to Android's system log, which the App does not store or send. If you use "Report a Problem" or the feedback options in Settings:

  • The App writes a plain-text message containing what you type (issue type, steps to reproduce and expected result, or your feedback), the App version, and your operating-system name and version. It does not include your PIN, profile names or financial records, but anything you type is included, so avoid entering sensitive information.
  • The App opens an unsent email draft to aksoft.support@gmail.com for you to review, edit or discard, or offers the share sheet if no email app is available. The App saves no copy.

If you email us, we receive what you send together with your email address and the details your email service attaches. We use it to respond and to investigate the problem. We do not set a fixed retention period for support emails, and a copy also stays with your own email provider.

Features

5Typed & Voice Entry

You can type a sentence such as "spent 450 on dinner yesterday" to log an entry. The App parses it on your device, sends it to no server or AI service, and shows you the result to confirm before saving.

You can also speak the entry, using the microphone permission. Before listening, the App checks that your device has an on-device speech recognizer (Android 12 or later) and asks it to use that recognizer. If none is available, voice entry does not start and the App does not fall back to an online recognizer; you can type instead. If recognition fails after starting, it stops with an error. The App does not save audio and does not send your voice or transcript to AKSoft Apps.

The recognizer is supplied by your device's operating system or manufacturer, so we cannot inspect what it does internally, such as how it obtains or updates language data. You can withhold the microphone permission and just type.

6Security & App Lock

  • PIN: If you set a PIN, the App stores a salted, one-way hash of it (not the PIN) in Android secure storage, which is backed by the Android Keystore. The PIN is never transmitted, is not included in backups, and there is no recovery mechanism that sends information anywhere. App Lock controls access to the App; it does not encrypt your files or backups.
  • Biometric unlock: Matching is done entirely by Android. AKMoney never receives or stores your biometric data.
  • Screen Protection: On by default for all users. While on, Android blocks screenshots of AKMoney and hides its preview in Recent Apps, and screen recording or casting may be hidden. It applies only to the App's own window, not to files you export or share. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Encryption: Each profile's database is encrypted. Receipt images, profile photos and reports are not encrypted; they rely on Android's app isolation.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7Backups & Restore

Manual backup. You can export a backup of the profile you are using: its financial records, templates, Financial Journey progress, currency setting, profile name and built-in avatar choice, and any receipt images. It does not include app-wide settings (theme, reminders, display name, App Lock) or custom profile photos. You set a password, and the file is encrypted with a key derived from it. AKMoney does not store the password; if you forget it, the backup cannot be restored.

You choose where the file is saved with the Android file picker, which may be local storage or a cloud-storage provider. AKMoney does not upload it automatically. The file name includes the profile's name (letters, numbers and dashes), which is visible wherever you save it.

Restore. You select a backup file and enter its password. A restore replaces the data in the profile you restore into and does not merge, so back up your current data first.

Automatic backup (Premium). Off until you enable it. It creates one encrypted backup per profile per day and keeps the three most recent, in the App's private storage. They are encrypted with a random key held in the device's Android secure storage, are not sent to any external storage, cloud service or AKSoft Apps, and are meant for restoring on the same installation. They may become unrestorable if you uninstall the App or that secure storage is reset. Deleting a profile deletes its automatic backups.

The App is configured to exclude its data from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so use manual backup to move data to another device.

8Receipt Scanning & Attachments

When you attach a photo to an expense, the App copies it into its private storage. It is not transmitted anywhere automatically and is not separately encrypted.

Receipt scanning (Premium). You take a photo with your device's camera app or pick an image from your gallery. The App does not declare the camera permission itself. Text recognition runs on your device (Section 3) and suggests details such as merchant, date, total and line items. Nothing is saved until you review the suggestions and save the expense. The reviewed details are stored in that profile's database, and the merchant name may be placed in the expense's notes.

Receipt details and images are included in backups. They are not included in PDF, CSV or Excel reports, though notes (including a merchant name) can appear in CSV and Excel exports.

Deleting an expense removes the record, but its image file may remain in the App's private storage until you remove the image, delete the profile, restore a backup into it, or clear the App's data.

9Other On-Device Features

  • Transaction templates: Reusable entry presets are stored in the local database and not transmitted.
  • Home-screen widget: Four fixed shortcuts (Add Expense, Add Income, Add Transfer, Open AKMoney). It shows no balances, amounts, history or templates and transmits nothing.
  • Financial Journey: Experience points, streaks and achievements are stored locally and not shared.
  • Milestone cards: Sharing is always your choice, through the Android share sheet. A card shows the milestone name and a message, XP or level figures if those toggles are on, and for a savings goal its name and progress percentage. Saved and target amounts appear only if you switch on "Show amount". The card is written as a temporary image file in the App's cache before sharing, which Android may clear later.
  • Reminders: The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled and delivered on your device.

10Reports, Exports & Imports

The App can generate PDF, CSV and Excel reports on your device, with no financial data sent to any server. They can include summary figures and individual transactions (date, account, category, amount). A PDF shows the profile's name and no notes. CSV and Excel exports include a description column, so free-form text you entered can appear in them.

The App does not upload reports. You choose where a CSV or Excel file is saved, "Share / Save PDF" opens the Android share sheet, and "Preview / Print" passes the PDF to Android's print system. Reports are ordinary unencrypted files, and the destination's own practices apply from then on.

When you import transactions from a CSV or Excel file, the file is read and checked on your device, you review the result before anything is saved, and the file is not uploaded.

11Premium & Google Play Billing

Premium features use Google Play Billing. Purchasing is not currently available: the Premium screen shows "Coming Soon", and Premium is active only for people who already have the entitlement (for example during Google Play closed testing). While the App runs, it asks Google Play about the Premium product and your purchase status, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. AKMoney never receives your payment card details and only reads back whether Premium is active.

Data control and legal notices

12Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is on your device, so you control it.

  • In the App: You can edit and delete records. You can delete any profile except your original one; this removes its financial data, receipt images, profile photo and automatic backups.
  • Everything: Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it removes all data the App holds on your device.
  • Files you saved or shared: Backups, reports and exports are not deleted by the above. Manage them where you saved them.

Because AKSoft Apps does not hold your financial records, we cannot look up, correct, export or delete them for you. You can turn off optional features (automatic backup, reminders, voice entry) or revoke their permissions at any time. Emails you sent to support are covered in Section 4; you can contact us about them, but deleting the App's data does not remove an email you already sent.

13Children & Legal Terms

Children. AKMoney is for general audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Anyone can still email us, and we would receive what they send; if you believe a child has done so, tell us at the address in Section 14. Data a child enters in the App stays on that device and can be removed by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

Legal disclosure and business changes. AKSoft Apps does not hold your financial records, but we may hold information you sent us, such as support emails, and may be required to disclose it under applicable law. Requests for data on your device or in your Google account would ordinarily go to you or to Google. If AKSoft Apps is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, support correspondence we hold may be transferred to the successor, subject to applicable law.
