1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AKMoney ("the App"), developed and published by AKSoft Apps ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information when you use the App. AKMoney is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy describes exactly what the App does, based on how it is actually built.

2 Information We Collect

AKMoney does not operate any backend server and does not automatically collect, transmit, or upload your financial data, personal information, or usage activity to us or to any third party.

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services that automatically send data off your device.

3 Information Stored Locally

To provide its features, AKMoney stores the following information locally on your device, inside the App's private storage:

  • Expenses, income, budgets, category budgets, accounts, transfers, recurring transactions, and financial goals you create
  • Receipt or attachment images you choose to add to an expense
  • Transaction Templates you create (see Section 13)
  • App preferences (such as theme, currency, and reminder settings)
  • Your Financial Journey progress (experience points, level, current and best streak, and achievement/milestone status) — see Section 9
  • Your App Lock PIN, if you set one, stored using your device's secure storage (see Section 10)

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted anywhere automatically. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data will remove this information from your device.

4 How We Use Information

All information described in Section 3 is used solely to provide the App's budgeting and expense-tracking features on your device (for example, calculating budgets, displaying transaction history, pre-filling transaction entry forms from your saved Templates, and generating the backup file described in Section 11). We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the App on your device.

5 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your information. We do not share your financial data with any third party. AKMoney does not include any advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK capable of automatically transmitting your data to us or anyone else.

The circumstances in which data may leave your device are all described in this policy and are limited to: actions you explicitly take to share information (Sections 7, 15, and 16); use of your device's speech-recognition service if you speak a transaction entry (Section 8); exporting or restoring a backup to a storage location you choose (Section 11); and Google Play processing an in-app purchase if and when purchases are available (Section 18). In each of these cases the action is either initiated by you or handled by platform services that you or your device operator control — AKMoney does not automatically push your data anywhere.

6 Third-Party Services

AKMoney relies on the following platform services to operate or to provide optional features:

  • Google Play Billing — to process optional in-app purchases, if and when available (see Section 18). AKMoney does not receive your payment card details.
  • Android operating-system share sheet — used only when you choose to share a diagnostic report, a financial report PDF, or a milestone card (see Sections 7, 15, and 16). AKMoney passes the content to the share sheet; the destination you pick determines which app or service receives it.
  • Device speech-recognition service — used only when you tap the microphone button to speak a transaction entry (see Section 8). This service is provided by your device's operating system or the device manufacturer, not by AKMoney.
  • User-selected file storage — when you use the Android file picker to save or restore a backup, you may choose a local folder or a cloud-storage provider such as Google Drive (see Section 11). AKMoney does not control that provider, does not receive your credentials for it, and does not upload backups to it automatically.

AKMoney does not integrate any advertising network, analytics platform, or crash-reporting service.

7 User-Initiated Sharing / Support Requests

The App includes optional "Report a Problem" and feedback features. These features do not send anything automatically. When you choose to use them:

  • The App creates a diagnostic file on your device containing: the App name and version, your device's operating system name and version, the App's internal database schema version, a count of your app settings, any description or reproduction steps you type, and the contents of a local error log (if one exists). This diagnostic file does not include your PIN, App Lock credentials, or your financial data (expenses, income, budgets, accounts, transfers, or goals).
  • The App then opens your device's standard share sheet so you can choose where to send this information (for example, your email app addressed to aksoft.support@gmail.com). AKMoney itself does not transmit this information anywhere — the choice of destination and the act of sending is entirely yours.

We do not store or retain a copy of any support request or feedback you send us outside of your own device and whatever inbox or service you choose to send it to.

8 Natural Language & Voice Transaction Entry

AKMoney lets you type a plain-language sentence (for example, "spent 450 on dinner yesterday") to quickly log an expense or income entry. This text is parsed entirely on your device by code built into the App — it is not sent to any server, AI service, or third party. The parser extracts fields such as amount, date, account, category, or income source from your sentence and always shows you the result for review before anything is saved, so you can correct or confirm it first.

You may also speak your entry instead of typing it. Tapping the microphone button requests the Android RECORD_AUDIO permission and uses the device operating system's speech-to-text service (via the speech_to_text plugin) to convert your speech into text, which is placed into the same text field and parsed the same way as typed text.

AKMoney requests on-device speech recognition from the operating system where the device supports it, so that recognized speech is processed without a network round trip. Whether this request is honored, and whether any audio or transcript data is handled by the device manufacturer's or Android's own speech-recognition components outside the App, is controlled by your device's operating system and installed speech-recognition service, not by AKMoney. AKMoney itself does not transmit your voice recording or transcript to its own servers or to any third party — we do not operate any server to receive it. AKMoney does not record or retain audio after a listening session ends; only the resulting recognized text is placed into the entry field, subject to the same local storage described in Section 3 once you save the entry.

9 Financial Journey (XP, Streaks, Achievements)

AKMoney includes an optional "Financial Journey" feature that tracks your consistency using experience points (XP), a level, your current and best day-streaks, and achievement/milestone badges (for example, a 90-day streak). These values are calculated from your own local activity (such as logging expenses or setting budgets) and stored locally on your device, in the same private App storage described in Section 3. They are not transmitted anywhere automatically, are not used for advertising or profiling, and are not shared with any third party.

10 App Lock and Security

If you enable App Lock, your App Lock PIN is stored using Android's secure Keystore-backed storage. Your PIN is never transmitted off your device, is not included in backup files (see Section 11), and there is no PIN-recovery mechanism that sends information anywhere. If you enable biometric unlock, biometric matching is performed entirely by your device's operating system — AKMoney never receives or stores your biometric data.

11 Backups and Restore

Manual (Portable) Backup. AKMoney lets you export a backup of your financial data — including your expenses, income, budgets, accounts, transfers, goals, and app preferences, as well as the actual bytes of any receipt images you have attached to transactions. You choose to export this backup yourself; AKMoney does not export or upload it automatically.

When you export a backup, you set a password to protect it. The backup is saved as a password-protected, encrypted file. The Android file picker is used to let you choose where to save it — for example, to local device storage or to a cloud-storage provider such as Google Drive. AKMoney does not control or operate that storage provider and does not receive your cloud-storage credentials. AKMoney does not automatically upload your backup to any AKSoft Apps server.

Your App Lock PIN is never included in a backup. If you restore a backup onto a device that does not already have an App Lock PIN set up, App Lock will be left off rather than restored in a state that could lock you out.

To restore a backup, you select the backup file through the device's file picker and enter the password you set when you exported it. AKMoney does not receive or store your cloud-storage credentials if you pick a file stored in a cloud service.

Automatic Backup. If you enable the Automatic Backup feature, AKMoney creates one encrypted backup per calendar day and keeps the three most recent backups. These automatic backups are stored entirely in the App's private on-device storage — they are not shared with any external storage, cloud service, or AKSoft Apps server. The automatic backup key is tied to your specific app installation; automatic backups cannot be transferred to another device or restored after reinstalling the App. For portable, transferable backups, use the Manual (Portable) Backup described above.

A backup may contain your financial information and receipt images. It does not contain your App Lock PIN.

12 Expense Attachments

If you attach a photo to an expense, the App copies that image into its private local storage on your device. Attachment images are not transmitted anywhere automatically and are only included in a backup file if you create one (see Section 11), which itself stays on your device unless you choose to move or share it yourself.

13 Transaction Templates

AKMoney lets you save Transaction Templates — reusable presets that pre-fill the transaction-entry form (for example, a "Morning Coffee" template that pre-selects a category and account). A template can store a name, transaction type (expense or income), an optional category or income source, an optional account, an optional amount, and an optional note.

Templates are stored locally on your device in the App's private database. They are not automatically transmitted to AKSoft Apps or to any third party. When you save or edit a template, the change is written only to your local device storage. Templates are available only inside the App and are not shown on, or accessible from, the Android home-screen widget (see Section 14).

14 Home-Screen Widget

AKMoney offers an optional Android home-screen widget with exactly four fixed actions: Add Expense, Add Income, Add Transfer, and Open AKMoney. Tapping an action opens the App to the corresponding screen; the widget itself does not create, edit, or store any transaction.

The widget does not display account balances, transaction amounts, account names, transaction history, Transaction Templates, or any other user-created or financial data — only the four fixed action icons described above. The widget does not transmit any information anywhere; it communicates with the App only through Android's standard widget update mechanism on your device.

15 Financial Reports and PDF Export

AKMoney can generate summary financial reports as PDF files. Reports are produced entirely on your device from your locally stored financial information — no data is sent to any server during report generation. A generated report can include summary figures (total income, total expenses, net cash flow, per-category and per-source breakdowns, and individual transaction entries by date, account, category, and amount). Transaction notes and other free-form text are not included in generated reports.

AKMoney does not automatically upload reports. When you tap "Share / Save PDF," the App opens the Android share sheet, and the report is passed to whichever app or destination you select. The privacy practices of that destination apply from that point forward.

Generating reports may require loading fonts. Where your device is online, the App may request a font from Google Fonts to improve the appearance of the PDF; if that request fails or the device is offline, the App uses a built-in fallback font instead.

16 Financial Journey Milestone Sharing

When you unlock a Financial Journey milestone or achievement, AKMoney offers an optional sharing feature. Sharing is always explicitly user-initiated — you navigate to the share preview screen and tap the "Share" button yourself.

The shareable card contains only: the milestone or achievement headline and name (for example, "30-Day Money Habit"), a motivational message, and optionally your XP or level figures if you choose to reveal them using the toggles on the preview screen. Transaction details, account numbers, and other financial records are not included in the shared content.

Sharing uses the Android native share sheet. AKMoney does not automatically transmit milestone information anywhere; the destination is chosen entirely by you.

17 Reminders

The optional daily expense reminder is a local device notification, scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No information is sent anywhere to enable or deliver reminders.

18 Purchases / Google Play Billing

AKMoney may offer optional Premium features through Google Play Billing. If and when purchases are available, Google Play processes the transaction — including your purchase information such as purchase status and product ID — in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. AKMoney does not process, receive, or store your payment card number or other payment credentials — these are handled entirely by Google Play. AKMoney only reads back your purchase entitlement status (for example, whether Premium is active) to unlock the corresponding features.

19 Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you control its retention. You can delete individual records within the App, or remove all App data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App from your device. Backup files you have created yourself are also stored locally and are subject to your own device's file management.

20 Children's Privacy

AKMoney is intended for general audiences managing their personal finances and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and since the App does not collect or transmit personal information to us in the first place, no such information is retained by us for users of any age.

21 Security

Your data is protected by your device's own operating system security and storage protections, and, where you choose to enable it, by App Lock (PIN and/or biometric authentication) as described in Section 10. Because AKMoney does not transmit your data off your device in the course of normal operation, your financial information is not exposed to network-based risks arising from our systems, as we operate none.

22 Lawful Disclosure

AKMoney does not hold your financial data on any server. Because your data is stored locally on your own device, we have no financial records to disclose in response to legal requests directed at us. If a legal process requires access to data held on your device, that process would be directed at you or your device, not at AKSoft Apps.

23 Business Changes

If AKSoft Apps is involved in a merger, acquisition, or similar business transaction, any user data we hold at that time could be transferred to the new entity. Because AKMoney is designed as a local-first application, we do not hold your financial records — those remain on your device at all times. Any such transaction would be subject to the privacy commitments in this policy.

24 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App. If we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

25 Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App, you can contact us at: aksoft.support@gmail.com
