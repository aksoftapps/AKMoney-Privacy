Privacy Policy

How AKMoney handles your information

AKMoney — Budget & Expense Tracker is a local-first app. Your financial records are kept on your device. This policy describes what the app does, including the optional features that use a network.

Last updated October 10, 2026

Key principles

A plain-language summary. The numbered sections below are the full policy and take precedence.

  1. Your records stay on your device. Transactions, accounts, budgets, goals and receipts are stored in the app's private storage. AKMoney has no accounts, and no AKSoft Apps server receives them.
  2. Nothing is sent to us automatically. AKMoney does not automatically transmit your financial records to AKSoft Apps. We may receive information you choose to send us, such as support requests, feedback, or diagnostic reports.
  3. No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. The app contains none. Technical errors are written to a local log on your device.
  4. You decide what you share. Backups, reports, exports, diagnostic reports and milestone cards go only where you send them.
  5. Optional features use a network. Google Drive backup (Premium) sends an encrypted file to your own Google Drive. PC Access (Premium) serves data over your local Wi-Fi to a computer you approve. Voice entry uses your device's speech-recognition service, which may process audio outside the app.
  6. Some technical requests go to Google. The app checks Google Play for your Premium status and, when you ask, for app updates. These contain no financial data, but Google receives standard connection information such as your IP address.

Overview

1Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AKMoney ("the App"), developed and published by AKSoft Apps ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information when you use the App. AKMoney is designed to keep your financial data on your device. The optional Google Drive backup and PC Access features (Sections 12 and 16) let you choose to send your data to your own Google Drive or to a computer you approve; AKSoft Apps does not receive it. This policy describes what the App does, based on how it is built.

2Information We Collect

AKMoney does not automatically transmit your financial records to AKSoft Apps. We may receive information you choose to send us, such as support requests, feedback, or diagnostic reports. The App has no AKMoney accounts and no cloud synchronization. Two optional Premium features move data in the ways described in their own sections: Google Drive backup (Section 12) signs in to your Google account and uploads an encrypted backup to your own Google Drive, and PC Access (Section 16) serves your data over your local Wi-Fi network to a computer you approve.

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. Apart from those optional features, the only network requests the App makes on its own are technical ones that do not contain your financial data: contacting Google Play to check the optional Premium product and your purchase status (Section 20), and, when you open Settings and choose to check for updates, asking Google Play whether a newer version is available (Section 6). Those requests are handled by Google, which receives the standard technical information that accompanies any such request, such as your device's IP address. If you set up Google Drive backup, Google likewise receives that technical information, your Google account details, and the encrypted backup file (Section 12).

Your data on your device

3Information Stored Locally

To provide its features, AKMoney stores the following information locally on your device, inside the App's private storage:

  • Expenses, income, budgets, category budgets, accounts, transfers, recurring transactions, fixed expense groups, tags, subcategories, and financial goals you create, kept separately in each profile you create
  • Profiles: the name and avatar of each profile (a built-in avatar, or a photo you choose from your device). Each profile keeps its own accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, recurring expenses, categories, and receipts, and switching profiles does not mix them
  • Receipt or attachment images you choose to add to an expense
  • Transaction Templates you create (see Section 14)
  • App preferences (such as theme and reminder settings, and the optional display name you enter for the greeting), and each profile's currency setting
  • Your Financial Journey progress (experience points, level, current and best streak, and achievement/milestone status) — see Section 9
  • Your App Lock PIN verifier, if you set a PIN, stored using your device's secure storage (see Section 10)
  • A local error log of technical errors, if any occur, and any diagnostic report files you generate (see Section 7)
  • If you set up Google Drive backup: the email address of the linked Google account, the time and size of the last upload, error status, a fingerprint used to detect whether your data has changed, and the encryption key material for the backup, kept in your device's secure storage (see Section 12)
  • If you use PC Access: the certificate and private key that PC Access uses for its local HTTPS connection and, if you turn on the optional authenticator code, its secret, kept in your device's secure storage (see Section 16)

Except for the optional Google Drive backup (Section 12) and PC Access (Section 16) features, which you must set up or start yourself, this information remains on your device and is not transmitted anywhere automatically. Each profile's financial database is encrypted on your device. Receipt images and profile photos are stored as separate files in the App's private storage and are not separately encrypted. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data will remove this information from your device; see Section 21 for what deleting a profile removes.

4How We Use Information

All information described in Section 3 is used solely to provide the App's budgeting and expense-tracking features on your device (for example, calculating budgets, displaying transaction history, pre-filling transaction entry forms from your saved Templates, and generating the backup file described in Section 11, or, if you choose to use them, the optional Google Drive backup and PC Access features in Sections 12 and 16). We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the App on your device.

Sharing and third parties

5Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your information. We do not share your financial data with any third party, except where you direct it to your own Google Drive or to a computer you approve through the optional Google Drive backup and PC Access features described below. AKMoney does not include any advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK capable of automatically transmitting your data to us or anyone else.

The main circumstances in which information may leave your device include:

  • files and messages you choose to share or save, such as reports, exports, backups and milestone cards, through the Android share sheet or file picker (Sections 11, 17 and 18);
  • diagnostic reports, feedback and support correspondence that you choose to send us (Section 7);
  • use of your device's speech-recognition service if you speak a transaction entry (Section 8);
  • uploading an encrypted backup to your own Google Drive and restoring it, if you set up Google Drive backup (Section 12);
  • serving your data over your local Wi-Fi network to a computer you have approved, if you use PC Access (Section 16); and
  • Google Play billing and update requests, which do not contain your financial data (Sections 2, 6 and 20).

AKMoney does not automatically transmit your financial records to AKSoft Apps. Information you send us yourself, such as a diagnostic report or support email (Section 7), reaches us only because you chose to send it. Once you have set up and switched on Google Drive backup, encrypted uploads to your own Google Drive run automatically, and you can switch them off or disconnect at any time (Section 12).

6Third-Party Services

AKMoney relies on the following platform services to operate or to provide optional features:

  • Google Play Billing — to check the optional Premium product and your purchase status, and to process purchases if and when they become available (see Section 20). AKMoney does not receive your payment card details.
  • Google Play update check — when you choose "Check for Updates" in Settings, the App asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. AKMoney downloads and installs nothing itself, and sends no financial data in this request.
  • Google Play Store listing — if you tap "Rate Us" in Settings, your device opens the App's Google Play page.
  • Android operating-system share sheet — used when you choose to share a diagnostic report, feedback, a report or export file, or a milestone card (see Sections 7, 17, and 18). AKMoney passes the content to the share sheet; the destination you pick determines which app or service receives it.
  • Device speech-recognition service — used only when you tap the microphone button to speak a transaction entry (see Section 8). This service is provided by your device's operating system or the device manufacturer, not by AKMoney.
  • User-selected file storage — when you use the Android file picker to save or restore a backup or export, you may choose a local folder or a cloud-storage provider such as Google Drive (see Sections 11 and 17). AKMoney does not control that provider, does not receive your credentials for it, and does not upload files to it automatically through this file-picker route. (The separate, optional Google Drive backup feature in Section 12 works differently.)
  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive — only if you set up the optional Premium Google Drive backup (see Section 12). You sign in with your Google account, and AKMoney uploads an encrypted backup to a private app-data area of your own Google Drive. Google handles your sign-in and stores the file under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

AKMoney does not integrate any advertising network, analytics platform, or crash-reporting service.

7Support Requests and Diagnostics

The App includes optional "Report a Problem" and feedback features. These features do not send anything automatically. When you choose to use them:

  • The App creates a diagnostic file containing: the App name and version, your device's operating system name and version, the App's internal database schema version, a count of your app settings, any description or reproduction steps you type, and the contents of a local error log (if one exists). A copy of this file is also saved in the App's private storage on your device. The diagnostic file does not include your PIN, App Lock credentials, profile names, or your financial records from the App's database (expenses, income, budgets, accounts, transfers, or goals). Anything you type into the description or steps is included, so avoid typing sensitive information there.
  • The local error log records technical error messages and stack traces from problems the App encountered. These are technical in nature, but we cannot guarantee that an error message never contains a fragment of the data being processed when the error occurred (for example, an amount or a note). The error log stays on your device. Entries older than 3 days are removed automatically, the log is kept to a limited size (the oldest entries are dropped first), and it is also removed when you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.
  • The App then opens your device's standard share sheet so you can choose where to send this information (for example, your email app addressed to aksoft.support@gmail.com). AKMoney itself does not transmit this information anywhere — the choice of destination and the act of sending is entirely yours.
  • The feedback and feature-request options share only the message you type, in the same way.

If you email us a report or request, we receive whatever you send, together with your email address and the other details your email service attaches. We use it to respond to you and to investigate the problem. AKMoney does not automatically transmit these reports; the local error log on your device and a diagnostic report you choose to send are separate things, and only the latter reaches us, and only if you send it. You can ask us to delete what you have sent (Section 21).

8Typed and Voice Entry

AKMoney lets you type a plain-language sentence (for example, "spent 450 on dinner yesterday") to quickly log an expense or income entry. This text is parsed on your device by code built into the App — it is not sent to any server, AI service, or third party by AKMoney. The parser extracts fields such as amount, date, account, category, or income source from your sentence and always shows you the result for review before anything is saved, so you can correct or confirm it first.

You may also speak your entry instead of typing it. Tapping the microphone button requests the Android RECORD_AUDIO permission and uses your device's speech-recognition service to convert your speech into text, which is placed into the same text field and parsed the same way as typed text.

Where speech is processed. AKMoney asks the operating system to use on-device recognition. This is a request, not a guarantee: which recognition service runs, and whether your audio or the resulting text is processed on the device or sent to a server, is decided by your device's operating system and the speech-recognition service installed on it (for example one provided by Google or your device manufacturer). On some devices, audio may be processed by that service's servers, under the privacy policy of its provider. If you do not want this, use typed entry instead; you can also withhold the microphone permission.

AKMoney itself does not send your voice or transcript to AKSoft Apps or to any server of its own, and does not record or retain audio after a listening session ends. Only the recognized text is placed into the entry field, and it is stored locally (Section 3) only when you save the entry.

9Financial Journey

AKMoney includes an optional "Financial Journey" feature that tracks your consistency using experience points (XP), a level, your current and best day-streaks, and achievement/milestone badges (for example, a 90-day streak). These values are calculated from your own local activity (such as logging expenses or setting budgets) and stored locally on your device, in the same private App storage described in Section 3. They are not transmitted anywhere automatically, are not used for advertising or profiling, and are not shared with any third party.

Security, backups and connected features

10App Lock and Security

If you enable App Lock, your App Lock PIN is stored using your device's secure storage. Your PIN is never transmitted off your device, is not included in backup files (see Section 11), and there is no PIN-recovery mechanism that sends information anywhere. App Lock controls access to the App; it does not encrypt your files, and your PIN is not used as the password for your data or your backups. If you enable biometric unlock, biometric matching is performed entirely by your device's operating system — AKMoney never receives or stores your biometric data. If App Lock is on, it is also used to authorize starting PC Access and approving a computer on your phone (Section 16).

Screen Protection. AKMoney has a Screen Protection setting that is on by default and is available to all users, not only Premium users. While it is on, Android blocks screenshots of AKMoney's window and hides AKMoney's preview in Android's Recent Apps screen (the preview is typically shown blank or black, depending on the device). AKMoney still appears in the Recent Apps list, and Screen Protection does not close the App. Screen recording or casting of the App may also be hidden. It applies only to AKMoney's own window: it does not affect files you export or share, notifications, the browser of a computer connected through PC Access (Section 16), or a physical camera. You can turn it off in Settings.

11Backups and Restore

Manual (Portable) Backup. AKMoney lets you export a backup of the profile you are using — including that profile's expenses, income, budgets, accounts, transfers, goals, recurring expenses, categories, templates, Financial Journey progress, and profile settings (such as its currency), as well as the actual bytes of any receipt images attached to its transactions. The backup also records the profile's name and built-in avatar choice. It does not include app-wide settings (theme, reminders, display name, Dashboard layout, or App Lock) or a custom profile photo. You choose to export this backup yourself; AKMoney does not export or upload it automatically.

When you export a backup, you set a password to protect it. The backup is saved as a password-protected, encrypted file. The Android file picker is used to let you choose where to save it — for example, to local device storage or to a cloud-storage provider such as Google Drive. AKMoney does not control or operate that storage provider and does not receive your cloud-storage credentials. AKMoney does not automatically upload your backup to any AKSoft Apps server. AKMoney does not store the password you choose; if you forget it, the exported backup cannot be restored. The file name includes the profile's name (letters, numbers, and dashes only), which is visible wherever you save the file. Once you save or share the file outside AKMoney, it can be copied or moved to other devices, and where it ends up is your choice.

Your App Lock PIN is never included in a backup, and restoring a backup does not change your App Lock settings.

Restore. To restore a backup, you select the backup file through the device's file picker and, for a password-protected export, enter the password you set when you exported it. AKMoney does not receive or store your cloud-storage credentials if you pick a file stored in a cloud service. A restore replaces the existing data in the profile you restore into; it does not merge the backup with existing data. You can restore into the current profile or, where your plan allows, create a new profile from the backup. Restoring never changes app-wide settings. The database is replaced in a single transaction, and receipt images are swapped in afterward, so make a backup of your current data before you restore.

Automatic Backup. If you enable the Automatic Backup feature, AKMoney creates one encrypted backup per profile per calendar day and keeps the three most recent backups for each profile. These automatic backups are stored entirely in the App's private on-device storage — they are not shared with any external storage, cloud service, or AKSoft Apps server by the Automatic Backup feature itself. (If you separately set up the optional Google Drive backup in Section 12, an upload of an encrypted copy of your data to your own Google Drive is attempted when you next open the App after a newer Automatic Backup exists, subject to the conditions in Section 12.) They are protected by your device's secure storage rather than by a password you choose, and are intended to be restored on the same installation of AKMoney on this device. They are not portable, and they may no longer be restorable if you uninstall the App or the relevant secure storage on your device is reset or lost. Deleting a profile also deletes that profile's automatic backups. For portable, transferable backups, use the Manual (Portable) Backup described above.

A backup may contain your financial information and receipt images. It does not contain your App Lock PIN. The App is configured to exclude its data from Android's own cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

12Google Drive Backup (Premium)

AKMoney includes an optional Google Drive backup feature. It is a Premium feature. New Premium purchases are not currently available (the Premium screen shows "Coming Soon"; see Section 20), but the App continues to recognise a Premium entitlement that Google Play already shows as owned, for example one held by a participant in Google Play closed testing, and Google Drive backup and restore are available to that user. It is not available to anyone who does not already have that entitlement. The feature does nothing until you sign in with your Google account and set it up.

  • Sign-in and access. You sign in with your Google account through Google Sign-In. AKMoney requests only Google's Drive "app data" permission, which gives it access to a private folder reserved for AKMoney in your Google Drive, not to your other Drive files. One Google account can be linked per device.
  • What is uploaded. An encrypted backup of all your profiles, in the same backup format and with the same kinds of content as the Manual Backup described in Section 11 (including receipt images attached to transactions). The Drive file has a fixed name that contains no financial information.
  • Encryption. The backup is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded, using AES-256-GCM with a key protected by a Backup Recovery Password that you choose. Google therefore stores only an encrypted file. AKMoney does not save the password itself. So that automatic uploads can run without asking for it, the App keeps the backup's encryption key material (which is protected by the password) in your device's secure storage. AKSoft Apps does not receive the file, the password or the key. Restoring on a device that does not hold that key requires the password; if you lose the password, an existing Drive backup cannot be opened on such a device, and you can reset the Drive backup and create a new one from the data on your device.
  • When uploads happen. An upload is made when you set up the feature, change the Backup Recovery Password, or reset the Drive backup. After that, uploads are attempted automatically when you open the App, if Automatic Backup (Section 11) is turned on and has completed a backup that is newer than the last Drive upload, automatic upload is switched on, you are still linked, a Premium entitlement is active, and the device is online. An upload is skipped when nothing has changed.
  • Storage and replacement. AKMoney keeps a single backup file in your Drive, replacing it on each upload (it may briefly hold an extra copy while it verifies an upload). You can switch automatic upload off at any time without disconnecting.
  • Restore. Restoring from Google Drive requires your Backup Recovery Password and replaces all profiles on the device; it does not merge with existing data.
  • Disconnecting. Disconnecting stops uploads and revokes AKMoney's Google authorization. You can choose whether to also delete the backup file from your Google Drive; if you do not, the file stays in your Google account until you remove it. Disconnecting works whether or not Premium is active. If Premium becomes unavailable, uploads pause and the file is left in place.
  • What Google receives. Google processes your sign-in, your Google account information, the encrypted file, and the standard technical information that accompanies any such request, such as your device's IP address, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. AKSoft Apps does not operate a server in this flow and has no access to your Google account.
  • What stays on your device. The linked account's email address, the time and size of the last upload, error status, a fingerprint used to detect whether your data has changed, and the backup encryption key material (in your device's secure storage). Technical failures are recorded without the contents of your data, but the caveat about the local error log in Section 7 still applies.

Google Drive backup is for backup and restore only. It is not cloud synchronization, and your data on the device remains the version the App uses.

13Expense Attachments

If you attach a photo to an expense, the App copies that image into its private local storage on your device. Attachment images are not transmitted anywhere automatically and are only included in a backup file if you create one (see Section 11), which itself stays on your device unless you choose to move or share it yourself. Attachment images are stored as separate files in the App's private storage and are not separately encrypted like the profile databases.

Deleting an expense removes the expense record; its attached image file may remain in the App's private storage until you remove the image from the expense, delete the profile, restore a backup into that profile, or clear the App's data.

If you choose a photo as a profile picture, the App keeps a processed copy in that profile's private storage. Profile photos are not included in backups.

14Transaction Templates

AKMoney lets you save Transaction Templates — reusable presets that pre-fill the transaction-entry form (for example, a "Morning Coffee" template that pre-selects a category and account). A template can store a name, transaction type (expense or income), an optional category or income source, an optional account, an optional amount, and an optional note.

Templates are stored locally on your device in the App's private database. They are not automatically transmitted to AKSoft Apps or to any third party. When you save or edit a template, the change is written only to your local device storage. Templates are available only inside the App and are not shown on, or accessible from, the Android home-screen widget (see Section 15).

15Home-Screen Widget

AKMoney offers an optional Android home-screen widget with exactly four fixed actions: Add Expense, Add Income, Add Transfer, and Open AKMoney. Tapping an action opens the App to the corresponding screen; the widget itself does not create, edit, or store any transaction.

The widget does not display account balances, transaction amounts, account names, transaction history, Transaction Templates, or any other user-created or financial data — only the four fixed action icons described above. The widget does not transmit any information anywhere; it communicates with the App only through Android's standard widget update mechanism on your device.

16PC Access / PC Manager (Premium)

PC Access (shown in the App as "PC Manager") is an optional feature that lets you use AKMoney from the web browser of a computer you approve, while the phone and the computer are on the same permitted Wi-Fi network. It is a Premium feature. New Premium purchases are not currently available (the Premium screen shows "Coming Soon"; see Section 20), but PC Access works for a user whose Premium entitlement is already active, for example a participant in Google Play closed testing. It is off until you start it in the App, and you can stop it at any time.

How it works and where data goes

  • When you start PC Access, your phone runs a small local HTTPS server on its private Wi-Fi address. It accepts connections only from devices on that same Wi-Fi network. Cellular, VPN, and phone-hotspot network configurations are not supported.
  • PC Access does not use an AKSoft cloud relay or backend. Data travels directly between your phone and the browser on your local network. Based on how PC Access is built, it does not automatically send PC Access data to AKSoft Apps' servers or to any third party. This statement is about PC Access only: the other ways data can leave your device, including Google Drive backup (Section 12), backups and files you export, and the items you choose to share, are described in their own sections.
  • Any device on the same Wi-Fi network may be able to load the sign-in page and its supporting files. That page does not contain your financial records, and no financial information is available until a computer has been paired and approved.

Pairing and approval

  • The phone shows a one-time pairing code that is valid for about two minutes. Each code allows a small number of wrong attempts, and repeated failures cause delays and then a lockout that you must clear on the phone.
  • After a correct code, you must approve the computer on your phone. The phone and the browser show a matching confirmation number to help you check that you are approving the right request. If App Lock is on, approving (and starting PC Access) requires your App Lock PIN or biometrics. If you have turned on the optional authenticator code, it is also required.
  • Only one computer can be connected at a time. Pairing is not remembered: every time you start PC Access, a new pairing is needed.

What a connected computer can see and do

  • View: your dashboard for the current month, transactions, accounts, categories, income sources, tags, budgets, goals, and insights for the active profile.
  • Change: add and edit expenses (including split expenses) and income, and import transactions from a CSV or Excel file. A browser cannot create or edit transfers, budgets, or goals. Receipt images are not displayed.
  • Imports: an uploaded file is processed by the App on your phone and held in memory only. Nothing from it is saved until you confirm the import. Pending imports are discarded when the session ends.
  • Information shown in the browser is held by that browser and computer while you use it. The App marks its responses as not to be cached and the page keeps only a cryptographic key, not your financial records, in the browser's storage. Ending a session stops further access but cannot erase what your computer has already received or displayed, so use PC Access only on computers and networks you trust.

Encryption and the certificate

The connection uses HTTPS (TLS) with a self-signed certificate that your phone generates, not one issued by a public certificate authority. Your browser will therefore show a warning the first time you connect. The App displays the certificate's SHA-256 fingerprint; compare it with the fingerprint in your browser's certificate details before you continue. Encryption does not protect you if you approve a connection from a computer or network you do not trust.

Session limits and when access ends

  • A session ends after 15 minutes without activity, or 8 hours after it was approved, whichever comes first.
  • It also ends immediately when you stop PC Access (in the App or from the notification), when the phone's Wi-Fi network or address changes, when you switch to a different profile, or when Premium becomes unavailable.
  • While AKMoney is locked by App Lock, PC Access refuses requests and a pending approval request is cancelled. If Android does not allow AKMoney to keep running in the background, PC Access stops shortly after you leave the App.

Android foreground service and notifications

While PC Access is on, AKMoney runs an Android foreground service (type "connected device") so the connection can keep working when the App is in the background. If you remove AKMoney from your recent apps, Android may stop AKMoney from keeping PC Access running in the background. This is separate from Screen Protection (Section 10), which only hides AKMoney's preview in the Recent Apps list; it does not remove AKMoney from Recent Apps and does not end a PC Access session. Android shows an ongoing notification ("PC access is active on your local Wi-Fi") with a "Stop PC Access" action. On Android 13 and later, AKMoney asks for notification permission when you start PC Access so this notification can appear. This notification is only a status indicator. Its presence or absence does not guarantee that the connection is secure or that no one else is connected, so check the PC Manager screen. The feature uses the Android permissions for foreground services, network-state access, and network access; it does not use your location or Wi-Fi network name.

What the phone keeps

While PC Access runs, the phone holds the pairing code and session details in memory only. These details include the local network address of the connected computer and a device label supplied by the browser (shortened and limited to letters, numbers, and a few symbols). They are discarded when the session ends or PC Access stops, and the App does not keep a history of connected computers. PC Access does not deliberately write these details to logs, and the caveat about the local error log in Section 7 still applies. Its certificate and private key, and the authenticator secret if you enabled it, are kept in your device's secure storage. They are not included in AKMoney backups (including Google Drive backups).

17Reports, Exports and Imports

Reports and exports. AKMoney can generate financial reports as PDF, CSV, or Excel (XLSX) files. Reports are produced entirely on your device from your locally stored financial information — no financial data is sent to any server during report generation. A report can include summary figures (total income, total expenses, net cash flow, per-category and per-source breakdowns) and individual transaction entries by date, account, category, and amount. A PDF report shows the name of the profile it was generated from and does not include transaction notes or other free-form text. CSV and Excel exports include a description column, so free-form text you entered on transactions can appear in them.

AKMoney does not automatically upload reports. When you save a CSV or Excel file, you choose the location with the Android file picker; when you tap "Share / Save PDF," the App opens the Android share sheet and the report is passed to whichever app or destination you select. Report files are ordinary, unencrypted files; the privacy practices of the destination you choose apply from that point forward.

PDF reports use a font bundled inside the App, so generating a report does not require a network request.

Importing transactions. If you import transactions from a CSV or Excel file, you select the file with the Android file picker. The file is read and validated on your device, and you review the result before anything is saved. The import does not upload the file anywhere.

18Financial Journey Milestone Sharing

When you unlock a Financial Journey milestone or achievement, AKMoney offers an optional sharing feature. Sharing is always explicitly user-initiated — you navigate to the share preview screen and tap the "Share" button yourself.

The shareable card contains only: the milestone or achievement headline and name (for example, "30-Day Money Habit"), a motivational message, and optionally your XP or level figures if you choose to reveal them using the toggles on the preview screen. Transaction details, account numbers, and other financial records are not included in the shared content.

Sharing uses the Android native share sheet. AKMoney does not automatically transmit milestone information anywhere; the destination is chosen entirely by you.

19Reminders

The optional daily expense reminder is a local device notification, scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No information is sent anywhere to enable or deliver reminders.

20Purchases / Google Play Billing

AKMoney includes optional Premium features that use Google Play Billing. Purchasing is not currently available in the App: the Premium screen shows "Coming Soon" and offers no buy or restore-purchase control. While the App runs, it uses Google Play's billing service on your device to check whether the Premium product is available and what your purchase status is; this request identifies the Premium product and is handled by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy, and it does not include your financial data. If and when purchases are available, Google Play processes the transaction — including your purchase information such as purchase status and product ID — in accordance with Google's own privacy policy.
